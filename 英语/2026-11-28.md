# 段落一
## 原文
When I was 27 years old. I left a demanding job in management consulting for a job that was even more demanding: Teaching. I went to teach seventh graders math in the New York City public schools. And like any teacher, I made quizzes and tests. I gave out homework assignments. When the work came back,  I calculated grades.
