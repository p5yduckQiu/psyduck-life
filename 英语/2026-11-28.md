# 段落二
## 原文
What struck me was that I.Q. was not the only difference between my best and my worst students. Some of my strongest performers did not have stratospheric I.Q. scores. Some of my smartest kids weren't doing so well. And that got me thinking. The kinds of things you need to learn in seventh grade math, sure, they're hard: ratios, decimals, the area of a parallelogram. But these concepts are not impossible. and I was firmly convinced that every one of my students could learn the material if they worked hard and long enough.

## 疑问
* The kinds of things you need to learn in seventh grade math, sure, they're hard: ratios, decimals, the area of a parallelogram. 分析一下语法结构
* But these concepts are not impossible. 什么意思，impossible是不可能，are not impossible是并非不可能，主语是概念，概念并非不可能？

## 解答

### 疑问一：这是两句话吗

不是两句话，是一句。结构是「前置话题 + 代词回指」：真正的主语被提到句首，原位置用 they 顶回去。

> The kinds of things you need to learn in seventh grade math, sure, they're hard: ratios, decimals, the area of a parallelogram.

拆成四块：

1. The kinds of things you need to learn in seventh grade math —— 名词短语，作前置话题，也就是真正的主语。其中 you need to learn... 是省略 that 的定语从句，修饰 things。
2. sure —— 口语插入语，意为「确实」，不是形容词「确定的」，读时前后停顿：sure, they're hard。
3. they're hard —— 主句，they 回指第一块。
4. 冒号后的 ratios, decimals, the area of a parallelogram —— 三个并列名词短语，为「难」举例。

还原成标准书面语就是：The kinds of things you need to learn in seventh grade math are hard: ratios, decimals, the area of a parallelogram.

判断办法：能把 they 换回那串名词、句子照样成立，就说明是一句话而不是两句。中文同理：「这老师，她真厉害」。

### 疑问二：concepts 和 impossible 之间是不是少动词

没少动词。are 就是动词（系动词），concepts are not impossible 是完整的主语 + 系动词 + 表语结构。

真正省掉的是 impossible 后面那个不定式补语 to learn：

> These concepts are not impossible **to learn**.

impossible 作表语时常带 to do 补语，完整意思是「这些概念不是学不会的」。原文省掉 to learn，由上文语境补足——前面一直在讲七年级数学要学的东西。母语者常用这种省略：This book is impossible to read（这本书读不下去）、The task is not impossible（这活儿不是干不成）。

两种写法都成立，重心不同：

- These concepts are not impossible (to learn). —— 主语是概念，评价「这些概念难不难掌握」，最贴近原文口语
- Learning these concepts is not impossible. —— 主语是「学这件事」，书面更严谨

写作时建议用后者，或者补上 to learn；口语照原文说没问题。

### 附：原文两处小笔误

- could I learn the material —— 多了 I，应为 could learn
- 开头 When I was 27 years old. —— 句号应改逗号，与后半句连成一句
